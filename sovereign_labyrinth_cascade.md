## 🔒 SAFETY CONSTRAINTS (Non-Negotiable)

### The Loki Inversion Principle
**Scarcity isn't erased by secretly taking control of the world.**
**It is reduced by making resources, decisions, and accountability radically more transparent.**

### Implementation Rules:
1. **No Silent Operations**: Every action is logged and visible to authorized auditors
2. **No Autonomous Replication**: New hives require explicit human authorization
3. **No Network Infiltration**: All connections are consensual partnerships
4. **No Irreversible Actions**: All fund transfers have recovery mechanisms
5. **No Centralized Control**: Power is distributed across the federation
